08 / Straight Answers

Frequently asked questions.

How do you deliver calculations faster and cheaper than traditional consultancies? +

Traditional consultancies route domestic jobs through account managers, graduate queues and manual report templates with high office overhead. At NeedACalc (part of Temporary Works Consulting & Design Ltd), you deal directly with Chartered Engineer David J. Miller using our streamlined digital scoping and Eurocode verification workflow — cutting turnaround to 24–72 hours and reducing fees by 35–55%.

Will Local Authority Building Control or my Approved Inspector accept the pack? +

Yes — our calculation packs are prepared specifically for Building Control submissions under Approved Document A, using current BS EN Eurocodes (BS EN 1990, 1991, 1993, 1995, 1996, 1997) and signed by a UK Chartered Civil Engineer (CEng MICE, Engineering Council 094002192) with £2,000,000 Professional Indemnity insurance. Reasonable clarifications requested by Building Control within the agreed scope are included.

Do your calculations cover temporary needling, Acrow propping and shoring? +

Yes. Because NeedACalc is a division of Temporary Works Consulting & Design Ltd, we explicitly address the temporary condition where relevant (needle beams, Acrow prop reactions, soleboard load spreading and dry-pack cure hold-points). For complex commercial temporary works, Cat 1/2/3 independent checks, crane mats or BRE 470 piling platforms, we provide full BS 5975 packages via temporaryworksconsulting.com.

How do fixed-fee quotes and Stripe payments work? +

You send your drawings, sketch or photos first. We confirm a fixed fee and delivery date in writing before any work starts. Once you accept, payment is made securely via a Stripe Checkout / Payment Link. We never store card numbers on this website.

What about the Party Wall etc. Act 1996? +

Where a beam or padstone bears onto a party wall, we provide the structural calculations, bearing stresses and reaction loads your Party Wall Surveyor needs for the notice and award. Formal notices and awards remain the responsibility of the building owner and an appointed Party Wall Surveyor.
