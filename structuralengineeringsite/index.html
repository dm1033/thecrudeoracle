Ask David a question ↗
When do I need a structural calculation for Building Control?+

Structural alterations such as removing a load-bearing wall, forming an opening, extending or converting a loft commonly need supporting structural information. Your building control body confirms the requirements. Building Regulations approval and planning permission are separate processes; a calculation does not provide either approval by itself.

How quickly can calculations be delivered?+

For straightforward, fully defined domestic work, we can discuss standard and priority delivery. A target programme is confirmed in your written quotation after the information and scope have been reviewed. Surveys, missing details, complex load paths and independent checking can affect the programme.

How does pricing compare with traditional engineers?+

Our digital workflow is designed to keep the service efficient and the fee clear. You receive a fixed written quotation for the agreed scope before committing. We do not claim that every job will cost less than every other consultant. Compare the scope, deliverables, checking and support as well as the price.

What about Party Wall Act requirements?+

The Party Wall etc. Act 1996 applies in England and Wales. We can provide structural details and reactions where included in the commission. The building owner remains responsible for the required process. Appoint a specialist Party Wall Surveyor for advice on notices, agreements, awards or disputes; our engineering commission does not automatically include those services.

Do you cover temporary works: needling, propping and shoring?+

Yes. Temporary works can be included as an expressly agreed, project-specific design scope. Safe support depends on the existing structure, loads, restraint, foundations or floors and the installation sequence. Generic notes and website diagrams are not a temporary works design or permission to proceed.

What software is used, and what will the outputs look like?+

Modern engineering analysis, calculation and drawing tools support the work, with software selected to suit the project. The issued pack can combine a clear design basis, calculations, labelled sketches, specifications and residual risk notes. Software output is professionally reviewed; the sample below illustrates the presentation, not a validated project design.

Is professional indemnity insurance held?+

Professional Indemnity insurance is held. All work is carried out by or under the direction of a Chartered Engineer. Ask for current evidence of cover and confirmation that your proposed scope is included in the written commission.

Are these calculations suitable for every UK project?+

The service focuses on domestic projects. The relevant jurisdiction, regulations and standards are confirmed for each commission; requirements differ across England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. Larger, unusual or complex schemes may require surveys, specialist input or additional independent checking.

When do I pay, and does payment approve the design?+

First we issue a written quotation and record your acceptance of its scope and terms. Any deposit or fixed fee is requested through a private quotation-specific Stripe Checkout invitation when configured. Payment does not approve a design for construction or confirm Building Control acceptance. The practice issues project-specific documents separately.
