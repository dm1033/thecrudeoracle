Governance & Data Protection

Privacy notice

Temporary Works Consulting & Design Ltd (“we”, Company No. 17198188) operates NeedACalc. This privacy notice covers enquiries and instructions regarding domestic and commercial structural calculations. Contact: david@temporaryworksconsulting.com or 07900 984900.

If you use the quote form, email us, or message via WhatsApp, we collect your name, email address, phone number, site postcode/area, project type, description, and any drawings or photographs you attach. We use that information solely to scope and quote your project and, if you instruct us, to prepare and issue your structural calculation pack. The lawful basis under UK GDPR is steps prior to entering into a contract and legitimate interests in responding to engineering enquiries.

Quote form submissions are transmitted via FormSubmit for onward email delivery to our practice inbox. Service acceptance is not a guarantee of inbox delivery. We never sell or rent enquiry data. Where you accept a fixed-fee quote, card payments are processed via a private quotation-specific Stripe Checkout invitation. We do not receive or store card numbers on this website. Stripe retains payment records including the quotation reference, revision, payment stage and evidence hashes. Written quotations and acceptance evidence are retained privately by the practice; these are not published in the website repository. The payment status endpoint exposes only a payment status, quotation reference, revision, stage and total to the holder of the payment reference. FormSubmit, Stripe, the hosting provider and WhatsApp process information where their services are used. Do not send payment card details or unnecessary personal information in enquiries or attachments.

Project drawings, calculation sheets and correspondence are retained for as long as necessary to deliver the service and comply with professional indemnity insurance and statutory record-keeping obligations. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data (where legal and insurance retention obligations permit) by emailing david@temporaryworksconsulting.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).
