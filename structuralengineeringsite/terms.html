Calculation terms & scope

These terms apply to domestic structural calculation services offered as NeedACalc by Temporary Works Consulting & Design Ltd (Company No. 17198188). Every written quote is an offer for a defined scope of elements. The practice records your written acceptance of the quotation reference, revision, scope, fee, terms and payment stage before issuing a private Stripe Checkout invitation. The written quotation states the total payable, tax treatment, inclusions, exclusions, programme, cancellation/refund arrangements and any deposit or balance. Enquiries and website fee estimates do not create a commission. The practice confirms when the work will start.

Calculations are design advice prepared for Building Control submission (Approved Document A). Site-specific dimension verification and Building Control acceptance are not guaranteed, though reasonable technical clarifications requested by Building Control within the agreed scope are included. You and your appointed builder remain responsible for verifying dimensions and existing conditions on site prior to ordering steelwork or cutting masonry, as well as compliance with the Party Wall etc. Act 1996. Appoint a specialist Party Wall Surveyor where formal notices or awards are required.

Designs are prepared in accordance with current Building Regulations, BS EN Eurocodes and relevant British Standards using the drawings and information you provide. Material changes to spans, layouts or loading after instruction may require a revised quote. We do not redistribute copies of copyrighted British Standards.

Outputs identify residual risks and temporary-condition considerations where relevant to needling, propping, shoring and structural alterations. Unless explicitly agreed in writing, a domestic NeedACalc pack is not a site-specific contractor method statement and does not appoint us as Temporary Works Coordinator (TWC) on site. Dedicated temporary works design, Cat 1/2/3 checking and RAMS support are available through temporaryworksconsulting.com.

Professional Indemnity insurance is held by Temporary Works Consulting & Design Ltd. Current evidence of cover can be requested with your quotation. Work is carried out by or under the direction of David J. Miller, MSc CEng, Engineering Council registration 094002192.

Payment and receipts: Payment is for the accepted quotation and specified stage only. Enter card details only on Stripe’s hosted checkout; do not email them. Stripe provides a receipt for a successful payment to the agreed receipt email. Quote reference, revision and payment stage link the payment to your commission. A receipt is not a VAT invoice; any required tax invoice is issued by the practice using its confirmed tax details.

Confirmation: Returning from Stripe does not itself confirm payment. The payment status page checks Stripe and the verified webhook record. Pending or delayed payments do not mean work has started. If confirmation is unavailable, contact the practice before paying again. Cancellations, variations and refunds are handled by the practice against the written commission and applicable rights; the return button does not cancel the commission.

Website examples — not for construction: The fee estimator, diagrams and sample sheets are demonstrations only. They are not checked or signed designs, Building Control submissions, temporary works permits or construction instructions. Do not select members, bearings, padstones, needles or props, order materials, remove walls or start work from these examples. Payment does not change their status. Only separately issued, project-specific documents may be used for their stated purpose and issue status.
