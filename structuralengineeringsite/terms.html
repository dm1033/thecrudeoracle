Terms of Engagement

Calculation terms & scope

These terms apply to domestic structural calculation services offered as NeedACalc by Temporary Works Consulting & Design Ltd (Company No. 17198188). Every written quote is an offer for a defined scope of elements. Design work starts when you accept that scope and pay the deposit or fixed fee requested via Stripe.

Calculations are design advice prepared for Building Control submission (Approved Document A). Site-specific dimension verification and Building Control acceptance are not guaranteed, though reasonable technical clarifications requested by Building Control within the agreed scope are included. You and your appointed builder remain responsible for verifying dimensions and existing conditions on site prior to ordering steelwork or cutting masonry, as well as compliance with the Party Wall etc. Act 1996. Appoint a specialist Party Wall Surveyor where formal notices or awards are required.

Designs are prepared in accordance with current Building Regulations, BS EN Eurocodes and relevant British Standards using the drawings and information you provide. Material changes to spans, layouts or loading after instruction may require a revised quote. We do not redistribute copies of copyrighted British Standards.

Outputs identify residual risks and temporary-condition considerations where relevant to needling, propping, shoring and structural alterations. Unless explicitly agreed in writing, a domestic NeedACalc pack is not a site-specific contractor method statement and does not appoint us as Temporary Works Coordinator (TWC) on site. Dedicated temporary works design, Cat 1/2/3 checking and RAMS support are available through temporaryworksconsulting.com.

Professional Indemnity insurance of £2,000,000 and Public Liability insurance of £10,000,000 are held by Temporary Works Consulting & Design Ltd. Work is carried out by or under the direction of David J. Miller, MSc CEng MICE, Engineering Council registration 094002192.
