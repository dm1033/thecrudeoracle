Design Brief ↗

Excavation support

Sheet piles, walers and struts hold the ground so the dig can continue safely below the first frame. We design and check the support, its installation sequence and the loads it carries.

Excavation support design →Illustrative scene, not a project design.

Working platforms

The crane and excavator stand on a prepared granular platform designed for the plant it carries. We design and check working platforms and piling mats, with the verification the site needs.

Working platform design →Illustrative scene, not a project design.

Structural stability

The steel frame stays braced and propped until its permanent stability system is complete, and each lift runs inside an exclusion zone with a banksman. We design temporary bracing, propping and restraint.

Propping & structural stability →Illustrative scene, not a project design.

Scope & competence note

Temporary Works Consulting & Design Ltd provides design, advisory, training and support services subject to agreed scope, competence requirements and project-specific information. Digital products, books, templates and tutoring support do not replace formal appointment, competent design review, independent checking, statutory duties or project-specific approval under the project temporary works procedure.
